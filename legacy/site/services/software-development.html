Our Custom Software Development Process

No two businesses operate the same way, and neither should their development process. We adapt our delivery model to your approval
cycles, compliance requirements, and stakeholder structures.

1

Requirements
Engineering

We start by understanding not just "what do you want to build", but why, for whom, and how it connects to measurable business outcomes. We document functional and non-functional requirements, integration constraints, compliance obligations, and success metrics before architecture begins. This is what most failed software projects miss.

2

System Architecture & Tech
Selection

Our TOGAF-certified architects design your system architecture, data models, API contracts, security layers, infrastructure topology, and scalability patterns. The tech stack is selected based on your team's existing skills, long-term maintainability, and project requirements, not general defaults.

3

UX Research & Product Design

Custom software fails when it's technically sound but nobody uses it. To ensure that doesn’t happen with your software, we validate workflows with real users before coding starts. User journey mapping, information architecture, and Figma prototypes are all tested with actual stakeholders. Your team signs off on UX before a single sprint begins.

4

Agile Development

Scrum, Kanban, or a hybrid, we work with whichever agile framework fits your team. Our two-week sprints include backlog grooming, sprint planning, weekly demos to stakeholders, and documented change control. You can reprioritize at every sprint boundary. Plus, you get full GitHub and Jira visibility throughout.

5

QA, Security and Compliance Testing

QA engineers are embedded from sprint one, not added at the end. Automated regression suites, manual exploratory testing, performance load testing, OWASP security scanning, and compliance validation (HIPAA, GDPR, SOC 2, PCI-DSS) run continuously, not just before release.

6

Deployment and DevOps

CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure-as-code, containerized deployments (Docker/Kubernetes), blue-green releases for zero downtime, and automated rollback. We hand over full infrastructure documentation and runbooks so your team can operate without us from day one, if needed.

What is custom software development?

Custom software development is the process of designing and building software specifically around a business's requirements rather than adapting an off-the-shelf product. It can help Australian businesses address unique workflows, integrate existing systems and scale their technology as requirements evolve.

When should an Australian business choose custom software instead of SaaS?

Custom software can be suitable when standard SaaS products cannot meet your specific workflows, integration requirements, security needs or scalability objectives. SaaS may be more appropriate when your requirements are standardised and you want faster deployment with predictable subscription costs.

How much does custom software development cost in Australia?

The cost depends on factors such as project complexity, features, integrations, technology stack, security requirements and development timelines. A simple application may require a significantly smaller investment than a large enterprise platform with multiple integrations and advanced AI capabilities. A detailed project assessment can provide a more accurate estimate.

How long does custom software development take?

Development timelines vary based on the scope and complexity of the project. An MVP or smaller application may take a few months, while a complex enterprise platform can take considerably longer. The timeline typically includes discovery, design, development, testing, deployment and post-launch support.

What technologies do you use?

We use modern technologies and frameworks based on each project's requirements. Our capabilities cover cloud platforms, web and mobile development, APIs, databases, AI and enterprise technologies. The technology stack is selected based on factors such as scalability, security, integration requirements and long-term maintainability.

Can you modernise our legacy application?

Yes. We can help modernise legacy applications through application assessment, code refactoring, architecture modernisation, cloud migration, API integration and user experience improvements. Modernisation can help reduce technical debt while improving performance, security and scalability.

Do you provide ongoing software maintenance?

Yes. Ongoing support can include application monitoring, bug fixes, security updates, performance optimisation, feature enhancements and technical assistance. Maintenance plans can be tailored to your application's requirements and business needs.

Can you build software for regulated Australian industries?

Yes. We can develop software for organisations operating in regulated sectors, with project requirements aligned to applicable security, privacy, compliance and industry requirements. Requirements are assessed based on the specific industry, application and data being handled.

Do you provide Australian business-hours support?

Support arrangements can be tailored to your project and operational requirements. Depending on the engagement, support can include defined service windows, application monitoring, incident management and ongoing technical assistance for Australian businesses.

Do you sign NDAs, and who owns the software you build?

Yes, we sign mutual NDAs before any discovery discussions. Custom-built software and code developed specifically for your engagement are fully owned by you on completion, with IP terms stated explicitly in every proposal.

How do I choose a custom software development company in Australia?

Shortlist partners with relevant industry case studies, recognised certifications such as ISO 27001 and senior architects in discovery. Ask for client references and a sample project plan before signing.
