Benefits of Microsoft Solutions

Getting the most from Microsoft is less about buying licences and more about how the platform is designed, governed and adopted. Here is what a well-run Microsoft environment gives your organisation.

Lower Cloud and Licence Costs

Budgets, tagging and right-sizing from day one, plus regular licence reviews, mean you pay for what you use rather than what was over-provisioned.

Better Collaboration

Microsoft 365, Teams and SharePoint give staff one place to work on documents, share knowledge and find information, wherever they are.

Faster Process Automation

Power Apps and Power Automate replace spreadsheets, paper forms and email chains with governed apps and workflows that teams can extend.

Stronger Security

Identity, access and data protection controls across Azure and Microsoft 365 reduce risk, with security mapped to the Essential Eight where you need it.

Connected Data and Insight

Dynamics 365, SharePoint and line-of-business systems feed Power BI, so leaders make decisions from the same trusted data.

Room to Scale

Azure services scale with demand, so new teams, products and locations can be added without re-platforming.

Our Microsoft Delivery Framework

We follow a structured six-phase framework for every Microsoft engagement, from the first assessment to day-to-day operations. Each phase has defined deliverables and checkpoints agreed with your team before work begins.

1

Assess

We review your workloads, licences, costs, dependencies and security posture across Azure, Microsoft 365 and on-premises systems, and agree the outcomes that define success.

2

Plan and
Design

We design the target architecture, identity and governance model, and the migration or build roadmap, with cost, security and data residency designed in.

3

Land

We set up a secure landing zone: identity, networking, policies, monitoring and cost controls, ready for workloads to move in safely.

4

Build and
Migrate

We migrate in waves with testing and rollback, and build what you need on top: SharePoint intranets, Power Platform apps, Dynamics 365 configuration and .NET applications.

5

Adopt and
Train

We train administrators and users, publish guidance for citizen developers, and support teams through go-live so new tools are actually used.

6

Operate and
Optimise

We monitor, patch and back up your environment with clear service levels, and review licence use and cloud spend with you regularly.
