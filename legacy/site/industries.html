Which industries does Q3 Technologies specialize in?

We specialize in 16+ industries including Healthcare and Life Sciences, Banking and Financial Services (BFSI), Manufacturing, Energy and Utilities, Retail and E-Commerce, Logistics, Education, and Travel and Transportation. With 30 years of domain experience, we deliver technology solutions tailored to the specific regulatory, operational, and business needs of each sector.

Does Q3 Technologies have industry-specific compliance expertise?

Yes. We have deep regulatory compliance experience across sectors — HIPAA and HITECH for healthcare, PCI-DSS and SOC 2 for BFSI, ISO 27001 for manufacturing, and FERPA for education. Compliance is built into every engagement from architecture through delivery, not added as a retrofit.

How does Q3 Technologies approach AI solutions for regulated industries?

We take a responsible AI approach for regulated industries — building explainable, auditable, and bias-aware models that meet sector-specific regulatory requirements. For healthcare, this means FDA-aligned AI validation. For BFSI, it means AML-compliant model governance. Our AI solutions are production-ready, not proof-of-concept.

What is Q3 Technologies' delivery model for enterprise clients?

We operate a flexible hybrid delivery model — onshore project leadership combined with offshore engineering capacity across India, UAE, and Australia. All engagements follow an outcome-driven framework with defined KPIs, transparent milestones, and dedicated client success management.

Can Q3 Technologies modernize legacy industry systems?

Yes. Legacy modernization is one of our core competencies. We have successfully migrated mainframe banking systems to cloud-native microservices, modernized on-premise ERP platforms for manufacturing clients, and re-platformed monolithic e-commerce systems to headless commerce architectures — all without disrupting live operations.
