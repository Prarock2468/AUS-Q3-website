What services does Q3 Technologies offer?

Q3 Technologies delivers seven core services: AI Solutions development , custom software development, BI and data analytics, cybersecurity, managed IT services, design (UX/UI/CRO), and software Testing and QA. We support enterprises end-to-end — from strategy and design to development, deployment, and 24×7 managed support.

Which industries do you specialize in?

We have delivered solutions across 16+ industries including banking and finance, healthcare and life sciences, retail and e-commerce, logistics, manufacturing, telecom, energy and utilities, real estate, media and publishing, education, automotive, gaming, sports, travel and hospitality, software and hi-tech, and industrial automation.

How do you ensure data security and compliance?

Our delivery is CMMI Level 3 assessed and ISO 27001 certified. We follow SOC 2–aligned controls and design every engagement with GDPR, HIPAA, and PCI DSS awareness depending on your industry. Security is embedded in our SDLC through DevSecOps, secure-by-design architecture, and continuous code and infrastructure scanning.

What engagement models do you offer?

You can engage Q3 through fixed-bid project delivery, time-and-materials, dedicated development teams (offshore or onshore), or outcome-linked commercial models. The right model depends on your risk appetite, roadmap clarity, and internal team structure — and we will recommend the fit honestly.

How quickly can you start an engagement?

For most projects, we can begin a structured discovery within 5–10 business days. Dedicated teams typically ramp up in 2–4 weeks depending on skill mix and clearances. Critical-path engagements can start faster on request.

Do you work with startups, or only enterprises?

Both. While our largest customers are Fortune 500s and global enterprises, we also partner with growth-stage startups — particularly in AI, fintech, EdTech, and healthtech — where engineering quality and speed-to-market are existential.

Where are you located, and which time zones do you cover?

We operate from India (Gurugram, Jaipur, Mumbai, Bengaluru), the US (Boston), the UK (London), and Australia (Melbourne). Our delivery covers all major time zones with 24×7 support coverage available for managed services customers.
