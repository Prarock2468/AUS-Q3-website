Our Story

Championing Quality, Shaping Tomorrow

  1. 1996

    Our Foundation

    Q3 Technologies’ global journey began with a landmark Internationalization (I-18n) project, establishing our commitment to quality. Our CEO, Anuj Mathur—a veteran engineering leader from several US multinationals—founded the company after experiencing firsthand how difficult it was to secure competitive software engineering quotes from large Indian IT service providers.

  2. 1998

    A Simple, Profound Idea

    Our inception was rooted in a simple yet profound idea: delivering high-quality, cost effective IT solutions to mid-size product and engineering companies around the world. From 1998 to 2005 Q3 continued to serve a growing client base in the US.

  3. 2000

    Bridging the Gap

    The year 2000 brought the Y2K wave, reshaping the Indian IT landscape. While the leading IT Solutions providers pursued financial giants in the BFSI domain, Q3 set out to bridge the gap between mid-sized U.S. companies in non-BFSI domains and reliable Indian IT firms that could solve complex product and software engineering problems.

  4. 2005

    Expanding in India

    To better leverage the Indian IT workforce, we established our first development center in Gurgaon in 2005. With a strong base in India, Q3 expanded its operations and delivery capabilities across the globe, with clients on every continent and offices in Germany, UK, UAE, Canada, and Australia.

  5. 2008

    Certified for Quality

    Achieved Microsoft Certified Partner status and obtained CMMI and ISO certifications, laying the foundation for our commitment to excellence in quality and standards.

  6. 2009

    Microsoft Gold Partner

    Elevated our status to Microsoft Gold Partner, reflecting our dedication to delivering top-tier solutions and services to our clients.

  7. 2010

    Jaipur Office

    Opening our Jaipur office marked a significant milestone in our expansion journey, strengthening our operational footprint and enhancing our service offerings.

  8. 2015

    Jaipur SEZ Office

    The establishment of our Jaipur SEZ Office further solidified our commitment to growth and innovation and bolstering our capabilities to better serve our clients.

  9. 2017

    Mumbai Office

    With the inauguration of our Mumbai office, we extended our presence to one of India’s key business hubs, reinforcing our commitment to serving clients nationwide.

  10. 2021

    ISO 27001 Certified

    Attained ISO 27001 certifications, highlighting our unwavering dedication to maintaining the highest standards of quality and security, and solidifying our reputation as a trusted technology partner.

  11. 2023

    Salesforce Partner and Australia

    Became a recognized Salesforce Consulting Partner, showcasing our expertise and expanding our service portfolio to offer innovative solutions to clients. Additionally, opened an office in Australia, marking another milestone in our global expansion journey.
